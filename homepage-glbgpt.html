06 Questions

Pinned up, answered plainly.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Not one line. You describe what you want in plain words and the workshop builds it. If you can write a sentence, you can build a site here.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Your first 3 builds are free and no card is needed. Plans start at $19/month only when you decide you want more.

What if a build comes out broken?

Then it's free. Broken output never costs you a credit — you only pay for work that works. And one-click rewind takes you back to any version that did.

Can I take my website with me?

Yes. Export your whole site at any time and host it anywhere. It's yours — Makeasy never holds your work hostage.

Does it work on my phone?

Fully. Describe, shape and publish from your phone, and every site Makeasy builds is made to look right on phones, tablets and computers alike.
