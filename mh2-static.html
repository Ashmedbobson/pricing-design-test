How it works

Three benches.
One walk down the floor.

No code, no setup, no waiting room. You describe it, you shape it, you publish it — the crew carries your page from bench to bench.

BENCH 01

Describe it

Type it, say it, or sketch it. Plain words are the whole instruction manual — the agent on this bench takes the order exactly as you give it.

"A bakery site with online orders, please."
BENCH 02

See it & shape it

Your site appears on the board in front of you. Tap anything to change it — words, colours, layout — and it moves the moment you do.

Keep scrolling — the tiles swap, the accent changes.
BENCH 03

Publish it

One press of the lever and it is live on the web, on every phone and computer. The stamp is the receipt — it stays yours.

The three doors

Three ways to build

However your idea arrives — as a sentence, a sketch in your head, or a picture you can see — Makeasy meets you there.

Sketch first

Before anything is built, you see the layout as a simple sketch — for 1 credit. Move things around, say yes, and only then does Makeasy build the real thing. No surprises, no wasted builds.

Your page, real & ready — LIVE

Talk it into shape

No menus to learn, no settings to hunt. You chat with Makeasy the way you'd talk to a person — type it, speak it, or start from a photo — and it follows you everywhere in the app, so help is never more than a sentence away.
