Makeasy · Homepage round

One homepage.
Five directions.

The same Makeasy homepage — same words, same sections, same pricing — built five ways, one for each design direction. Every page is clickable: scroll it, drag it, tap it. Pick the one that feels like Makeasy.

Direction 01 · Kiln

Scroll to build

Scroll and a rough sketch heats up stage by stage — idea, draft, colour, polish — until it glows and goes live.
Direction 02 · Fathom

Descend through a build

Scroll down 200 metres through a build: pages, features and readiness gauges climb as you sink to rated depth.
Direction 03 · Pressroom

Drag the roller

Drag across a blank sheet and the finished homepage inks into view, pass by pass. Then write your build ticket.
Direction 04 · Orchard Row

Pick a path, fill a crate

Choose Launch, Sell, Book or Grow, then tap the features you want into your crate and see which plan fits.
Direction 05 · Static

Assemble in rhythm

Tap the step sequencer — Hero, Features, Booking — and hear and see a page assemble, beat by beat.
