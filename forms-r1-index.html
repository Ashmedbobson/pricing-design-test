Forms App — Design Round 1

Five directions for how the app is arranged. Every one carries the same workspace, the same forms and the same five templates — judge only the design. Tap one to open it, use your browser's back button to return here.

Direction 1
Ledger

One long, beautifully typeset document. No tabs — everything in order, like a well-kept register. Serif headlines, ink on paper.

Desk

An organiser's dashboard: the big responses number up top, action tiles, compact lists. A working tool, data-forward.

Direction 5
Stream

Activity first: latest responses as a friendly feed, templates on a swipeable shelf. Warm and alive.

Nothing here is applied to the live app. Pick a winner — or a mix — and that becomes the design the app is built to.
